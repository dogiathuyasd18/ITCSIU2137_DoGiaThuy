Customer User Manual

Getting Started
To use the Tourism Website, create an account. Click "Register" or "Sign Up" on the homepage, then provide your email, password, first name, last name, phone number, address, and gender. After registration, you are logged in automatically. To log in later, click "Login" or "Sign In", enter your email and password, and click "Login". You can log out by clicking your profile icon in the header and selecting "Logout".
Browsing and Searching Products
Browse tours on the homepage or go to the Products page. Use the header search bar to find tours by keywords (e.g., "Paris", "Beach"); results appear as you type. Click a tour card or search result to view details, including name, description, price, available dates, ratings, reviews, country, and category. Select a travel date if available.

Making Bookings
To book a tour, go to the tour detail page, select a travel date, number of travelers, and optional special requests, then click "Book Now" or "Add to Cart" to open the booking form. Review the booking details (tour info, selected date, quantity, total price), add special requests if needed, select a payment method, and click "Confirm Booking". You will be redirected to complete payment via MoMo or Credit Card, and receive a confirmation after successful payment.

Managing Your Bookings
Access "My Bookings" from the header to view your bookings with booking ID, tour name, travel date, status (Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled), and total amount. Click a booking to view full details, including payment status, travel schedule, and special requests. You can cancel a booking if allowed (restrictions may apply based on travel date), update payment status, or view receipts from the booking details page.

Reviews and Feedback
After completing a tour, you can submit a review. Go to "My Bookings", find a completed booking, click "Write Review", rate the tour (1-5 stars), write your review, and submit. You can also complete surveys by navigating to "Survey" from the menu, answering the questions, and submitting your responses.

Profile Management
Click your profile icon to view your account information. From profile settings, you can update your name, phone number, address, and password to keep your account information current.

Admin User Manual

Admin Access and Dashboard
Admins log in with admin credentials and are redirected to the Admin Dashboard, which provides an overview of statistics, key metrics, and quick access to management functions. The dashboard shows total revenue, booking counts, user statistics, and popular products.

Product Management
The Products section lists all tours with SKU, name, and price, and includes search and filter options. To create a product, click "Add New Product" and enter the product name, SKU, price, description, category, and country, then click "Create Product". To update, find the product, click "Edit", modify fields, and click "Update Product". To delete, find the product, click "Delete", and confirm. Note that products with existing bookings may have deletion restrictions. Travel schedules are managed in Admin → Orders (Time Travel), where you can view schedules by product and update start date, end date, and available quantity, then click "Update Schedule".

User Management
The Users section lists all users with email, name, and role, and includes search and filter options. Click a user to view profile information, booking history, and account status. User roles (Admin/Customer) are displayed, though role changes may require system-level access.

Order Management
The Orders section shows all bookings/orders with status (Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled), customer information, and payment status. Click an order to view customer details, product information, travel dates, payment information, and special requests. From order details, you can update status by confirming bookings, marking as completed, or canceling orders, then save changes.

Analytics and Reporting
The Dashboard shows total revenue, total bookings, user statistics, and popular products. The Advanced Analytics section provides Statistical Analysis, Crosstab Analysis, Chi-Square Analysis, User Analytics, Price Optimization suggestions, and Growth Forecasting. Charts are available in Admin → Chart or Analytics, showing revenue charts, booking trends, user activity, and product performance. Price Elasticity Analysis calculates elasticity metrics from historical data to inform pricing decisions. Price Optimization provides suggestions based on historical data, elasticity calculations, and demand patterns.

Data Management
Tourism data can be imported via Analytics by clicking "Import Tourism Data", uploading a CSV file, and verifying the import. The system manages available years for analysis, allowing you to select specific years when generating reports and forecasts.

A/B Experiment Management
Price experiments are configured in Admin → Analytics → Experiment settings. Set the experiment percentage (traffic allocation) and active/inactive status, then save. Experiment Reports show exposure statistics, conversion rates, revenue comparisons between control and experimental groups, and statistical significance to evaluate experiment effectiveness.

System Configuration
Product details can be updated in Admin → Update by selecting a product and modifying product information, pricing, descriptions, and categories. Travel times are managed in Admin → Update Detail, where you select a product schedule and update travel dates, availability, and pricing for specific dates.
Common Features and Navigation
Both customer and admin users benefit from consistent navigation features. The header menu provides quick access to main sections, while admins have an additional sidebar for navigation. Breadcrumbs help track your location. Global search allows finding products quickly, with filter and sort options. The system displays success messages for completed actions, error messages for issues, and status updates for ongoing processes. The website is responsive and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
